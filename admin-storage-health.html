How this page is calculated (click to expand)

ItemSource / query / rule
Pool metricsOne query per metric for all pools: last_over_time(ceilometer_volume_provider_pool_capacity_{total,allocated,provisioned,free,virtual_free}[10m]). The current panel runs 3 queries per pool and reads only the current instant.
Physical useallocated ÷ total. Warning ≥ 75%, critical ≥ 90%.
Virtual useprovisioned ÷ limit, with limit = provisioned + virtual free (total × max_over_subscription_ratio). Same thresholds. On thin pools this is usually what runs out first.
Forecastpredict_linear(…[7d], …) on both free and virtual free; the page shows whichever reaches zero first. Critical ≤ 14 days, warning ≤ 30 days. Needs 7 days of history. The current panel predicts free only, which on thin pools stays at total and never warns.
Stale poolNo sample in the last 10 min but one in the last 24 h. Its values are frozen at the last report.
Needing attentionBlock Storage API, all projects. Critical: error, error_deleting, error_restoring, error_extending. Warning: creating, attaching, detaching, deleting for more than 30 min. Info (clean-up): unattached for 30 days or more, snapshots older than 90 days with a newer one of the same volume.
Services, backends, projects, imagesceilometer_volume_service_health; pools summed per backend; sum by (project) of volume and snapshot sizes; ceilometer_image_size, image_download, volume_backup_size.
Refresh and errorsEvery 60 s through the shared cache (the current panel refreshes cards every 10 s). If Aetos fails, the last good data is shown with its age instead of the raw exception text.

Thresholds can be overridden in local_settings with GRIAN_PLUGIN['storage_thresholds']. The panel keeps its URL (/admin/volume_metrics/) so existing links and bookmarks still work.
